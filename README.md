# Maps chemist-level sales data back to probable prescribing doctors, giving pharma field sales teams actionable doctor-level prescription intelligence without needing direct prescription data.


## About

In pharmaceutical sales, the most critical — and most difficult — metric to track is actual prescription behaviour at the doctor level. Since prescription data is rarely accessible directly, the next best signal lives at the chemist: classically, 2–3 chemists within a doctor's consulting catchment area absorb the majority of that doctor's prescriptions.

RxTrace operationalizes this insight by mapping geospatial proximity between doctor clinics and chemist outlets, weighting chemist sales data by proximity, and generating a **Doctor Prescription Proxy Score** per molecule per month. The result is a territory-level intelligence tool that helps field sales teams prioritize rep visits, detect competitive displacement early, and identify untapped high-volume doctors — all from data that already exists.
